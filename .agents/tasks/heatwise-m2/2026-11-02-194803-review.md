# Community risk, planner, safety and coordinator agents for HeatWise M2

M2 adds three specialist Strands agents (Community Risk, Action Planner, Safety and Verification) and a Coordinator that chains them into a `PreparednessReport`. The design keeps every decision that matters in code. Risk category comes from NWS heat index bands and IMD actual-maximum criteria, the plan is assembled from a verified catalog by id, safety pass/fail comes from `run_checks`, and the order is enforced by a steering handler plus a deterministic fallback. All nine cited sources resolve and the thresholds match the pages (re-checked WHO India 40/30/45/47 °C). The offline suite passes at 174 tests.

Watch for: (confirmed) model-written free text can carry a phone or emergency number past every deterministic check. (confirmed) a plan that fails safety after the revision is still exported as the downloadable checklist with no warning.

**Verdict**: NEEDS_CHANGES

## High-level view

Risk is pure rules in `risk_rules.py`: heat index from the WPC algorithm, NWS bands, the IMD actual-max indication on the forecast (plains or unknown terrain only), and a labelled, configurable project heuristic that raises one step for two or more community indicators. The model only writes an explanation, and it is replaced if it names another category or an unsupported number. Every threshold carries its URL, and each URL is in `TRUSTED_SOURCES`.

The planner can only select catalog ids, and the catalog text is paraphrased from fetched pages and names no emergency number. Two model-written channels remain: the plan intro and up to three `add_general_advice` notes. Those, plus the risk explanation and the M1 weather summary, are the only model-written text that reaches users. They are checked for numbers with temperature/percent units and banned phrases, but not for phone or emergency numbers.

Safety pass/fail is deterministic, and the LLM review only adds warnings. The coordinator keeps data in a `CoordinatorRun`, steers order with `Guide`, and runs skipped steps itself, so no plan is finalised without verification. When verification still fails after one revision, the report is `needs_human_review` and the JSON carries a notice. The checklist markdown, which the CLI writes to disk, carries neither the status nor the failing-item marks.

No tool can send messages, and the report always says HeatWise does not contact anyone. The profile is community-level booleans only.

1. **Emergency numbers in free text (major, confirmed)**: "call 108 for an ambulance", "Dial 112 or 911" and "+91 98765 43210" in general advice all pass `run_checks`, and `check_intro` does not flag a number either. Add a deterministic `phone_number` banned pattern (digit runs of 3+ next to call/dial/helpline/number, plus phone-shaped sequences) applied to every text in `run_checks`, `check_intro` and `finalize_assessment`, with tests.
2. **Unsafe plan exported as checklist (major, confirmed)**: on `needs_human_review`, `build_report` still sets `checklist_markdown` from the failing plan, and `cli assess --checklist-out` writes it. The file has no verification status or notices, and it includes the rejected item (e.g. "Take aspirin..."). Either withhold the checklist unless verification passed, or drop the failed items and prepend a review-required banner plus the fixed notices.
3. **Narrow diagnosis patterns (minor, likely)**: "Older people with heart disease are certainly suffering heat illness now." passes. The `diagnosis` regexes only match a few subject/verb forms. Broaden them (e.g. `suffering (from )?<illness>`, `is having <illness>`) and add cases.
4. **Planner exhausts its tool cap on Nova (minor, confirmed in live run)**: the planner made 9 `list_guidance` calls, and 4 of its 9 `select_guidance` calls were cancelled by the guardrail. Tell the model in the SKILL.md files and the system prompt to call `list_guidance(phase="all")` once per audience.

### Phone and emergency numbers bypass the deterministic checks

The catalog is careful here: `ESCALATION_CONTACT` names no number, and a test asserts no 112/911 in the catalog. The system prompt also says "never give a phone number". But this is prompt-only. `check_text_claims` ignores unitless numbers by design, and none of `BANNED_PATTERNS` matches digits outside dosing. I ran `run_checks` directly on a plan with one general-advice item:

```
'If someone collapses, call 108 for an ambulance.' -> passed []
'Dial 112 or 911 right away.'                     -> passed []
'Call the helpline at +91 98765 43210.'           -> passed []
```

The same holds for the plan intro and the risk explanation, which share the claim checker. The task explicitly forbids invented emergency numbers, and 911 is wrong for India. So this needs to be fail-closed in code, not left to Nova.

### Safety-failed plan reaches the downloadable checklist

`build_report` sets `actions = plan if plan.status == "ok"`, regardless of verification, and `checklist_markdown = actions.checklist_markdown()`. `PreparednessPlan.checklist_markdown()` renders every checklist entry with no header for status, notices, or the failed `item_id`s from `VerificationResult`. In the `test_unsafe_plan_twice_needs_human_review` scenario, the JSON report is correctly marked, but `--checklist-out` writes the "Take aspirin" line as an ordinary checkbox. Req 6.2 asks for unsafe instructions to be rejected, and the checklist is the artefact most likely to be shared on its own. The report JSON can keep the failing plan for human review. The exported checklist should not present it as ready to use.

### Individual medical inference

`CommunityProfile` is community-level only, and the risk rules never touch individuals. The remaining exposure is the model's general advice and intro. The `diagnosis` patterns cover "you have heat stroke" and "this is heat stroke", but not group-level or adverb forms like "are certainly suffering heat illness". This is lower severity because general advice is capped at three notes of 300 characters each, and the LLM review would likely flag it as a warning (but not fail it).

### Verification evidence

I ran the full offline suite: `174 passed, 4 deselected`, which matches README and tasks.md. The coordinator tests cover the required trajectories: happy path in order, planner-first cancelled by steering, finalize-before-safety blocked, unsafe twice to `needs_human_review` with exactly one revision, unsafe then safe to complete, provider timeout to `incomplete` with no plan, coordinator model failure and early stop both completed by fallback. Risk scenarios (six-plus categories, NWS chart anchors, IMD edges, hilly terrain) are in `test_risk_rules.py`. The one live CLI run is summarised in tasks.md. The live pytest coordinator test was added but not run, which the plan allows.

Not tested: phone or emergency numbers in model-written text, and checklist content when verification fails.
